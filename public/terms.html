Terms of Service & Limitation of Liability

Effective Date: November 2026 • Governing Law: Jurisdiction of Courts at Jaipur & New Delhi, Republic of India

1. Software Platform Role (Evidence Witness, Not Inspection Agency)

Padavo Technologies Private Limited (“Padavo”) provides an enterprise milestone verification, cryptographic audit trail, and communication software platform. Padavo is not an authorized cargo surveying firm, third-party inspection agency (e.g. SGS, Bureau Veritas), or goods quality guarantor.

All photos, visual records, laboratory certificates, and telemetry data ingested through WhatsApp, vendor portals, and camera links are provided by the respective Exporters, Subcontractors, and Customs House Agents. Padavo records the tamper-proof cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of such records at the exact timestamp of transmission but does not physically verify, sample, or certify the physical condition or chemical properties of physical consignments.

2. Complete Limitation of Financial & Maritime Liability

Under no circumstances shall Padavo, its directors, employees, or infrastructure providers be liable for:

  • Cargo Rejection or Buyer Commercial Disputes: Any claim, chargeback, or refusal of payment by overseas buyers arising from goods shade variations, sizing discrepancies, moisture retention, or fabric hand-feel.
  • Maritime Demurrage & Port Delays: Detention charges, port storage penalties, unseasonal rainfall yard drying delays, or customs inspection hold-ups.
  • Foreign Exchange Fluctuations: Interbank rate movements occurring between milestone sign-off and physical bank wire settlement.
Maximum Aggregate Liability: In all cases, Padavo's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to these services shall be strictly capped at the subscription fees actually paid by the exporter in the preceding three (3) months.

3. RBI & FEMA Non-Custodial Declaration

In compliance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Payment Aggregator and Payment Gateway (PA/PG) guidelines, Padavo does not custody, pool, or hold third-party buyer or seller wire funds in any escrow account. All payments are executed directly between the foreign buyer's bank and the exporter's bank via Authorized Dealer Category-1 (AD Cat-1) institutions and licensed international escrow partners (e.g. Tazapay / Wise).

4. Electronic Evidence Admissibility (Section 65B BSA 2023)

Padavo's dispute evidence certificates and milestone audit logs are formatted to satisfy the evidentiary standards of Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 and Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. These electronic records are contemporaneous, server-clock synchronized, and tamper-evident for arbitral tribunal and court submission.
