Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before onboarding your foreign buyers.

Does my overseas buyer need to install an app or create a password?
No. Foreign buyers get an encrypted, zero-login public URL (e.g. padavo.app/track/rj-pouk8492). It opens immediately in any browser on iPhone, Android, Mac, or Windows PC.
Does Padavo hold our money like an escrow service?
No. Padavo does not custody foreign exchange or cross-border funds, avoiding RBI payment aggregator regulations. Padavo provides the cryptographic sign-off verification that unlocks your verified bank wire (SWIFT & IBAN) coordinates, and the buyer wires payment directly to your account.
What if our factory basement or dye yard has weak internet?
Padavo automatically compresses high-resolution photos on the worker's device down to ~240KB before sending. If connection drops completely, our offline queue saves photos on the phone and automatically syncs when the worker reconnects to Wi-Fi.
Can our floor supervisors upload directly through WhatsApp?
Yes. You can generate a "WhatsApp Magic Token Link" for each order. Floor staff (like Mukesh or Ramu) tap the link directly inside WhatsApp, take a photo, and it instantly syncs to the order timeline with zero app download.
