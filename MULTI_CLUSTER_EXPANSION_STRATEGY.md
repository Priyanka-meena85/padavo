## "TraceCraft / KarkhanaFlow" — Dynamic Production Tracking for Indian Export Hubs

## 1. The Core Multi-Cluster Thesis

Moving beyond Jaipur requires an **Industry-Agnostic Workflow Engine**. While the factory materials differ (cotton vs. sheesham wood vs. brass vs. leather), the underlying exporter-buyer dynamics across India's top export clusters are **100% identical**:

1. **Custom Batch Production (MTO / OEM / ODM):** Goods are produced after PO confirmation; not picked off a warehouse shelf.
2. **High Order Value:** Shipments range from ₹5 Lakh to ₹50 Lakh+ per container.
3. **Long Lead Times (30 to 90 Days):** Multi-week gaps create extreme anxiety for foreign buyers.
4. **Scattered Subcontracting (Job-Work):** Work moves between specialized local artisans (tanners, carvers, dyers, polishers, casters).
5. **The WhatsApp Black Hole:** Crucial QC photos and milestone updates get lost in unorganized chat histories.

### Critical QC Evidence Required Per Cluster

1. **Textiles (Jaipur / Tirupur / Surat):**
   * *Pantone TCX swatch comparison under standard D65 daylight.*
   * *Tape measurement across critical specs (chest width, body length).*
   * *Print bleed and registration check.*
2. **Solid Wood Furniture (Jodhpur / Saharanpur):**
   * *Digital Pinless Moisture Meter photo (verifying moisture content is strictly between 8%–12% to prevent warping in dry US/EU winters).*
   * *Joint dovetail / mortise-tenon alignment photo.*
   * *Corner drop-test on inner corrugated packaging.*
3. **Metalware & Brass (Moradabad):**
   * *Micron thickness photo for electroplating (Gold/Nickel/Antique Brass).*
   * *Lacquering smoothness under inspection lamp (zero pinholes/oxidation).*
   * *Carton vibration and bursting strength (B.F.) test.*
4. **Leather Goods & Footwear (Agra / Kanpur / Ambur):**
   * *Hide grade inspection (zero scars, uniform pebble/smooth grain).*
   * *Insole bonding and seam tensile pull test.*
   * *Sole flex test photo and inner barcode scan.*
5. **Handmade Carpets (Bhadohi / Mirzapur):**
   * *Knot density count per square inch (KPSI check with counting glass).*
   * *Pile height gauge measurement.*
   * *Backing latex curing and fringe binding finish.*

### Key Data Model Enhancements
To support any cluster seamlessly:

1. **`Industry_Cluster` Entity:**
   * `id`, `name` (e.g., "Textile & Apparel", "Solid Wood Furniture", "Metal & Hardware", "Leather & Footwear", "Carpets & Rugs", "Custom")
   * `default_stages`: Array of JSON objects `[{ name: "Cutting", default_icon: "scissors", required_photos: 2 }]`
   * `qc_metrics_schema`: JSON definition of cluster-specific measurements (e.g. `["Moisture Content %", "GSM", "Leather Grade"]`).

2. **`Order` Entity:**
   * `id`, `exporter_id`, `buyer_name`, `buyer_country`, `product_name`, `cluster_type`
   * `custom_stages`: Inherited from preset, fully re-orderable, editable, or appendable by the exporter.
   * `status`: `Draft`, `In Production`, `QC Approved`, `Packed`, `Dispatched`.
   * `buyer_token_slug`: Obfuscated unique public URL (e.g. `/track/jodhpur-artisan/po-8419-f9c3a`).

3. **`Stage_Update` Entity:**
   * `id`, `order_id`, `stage_name`, `stage_index`, `status` (`pending`, `in_review`, `approved_by_owner`, `published`)
   * `photos`: Array of URLs with timestamps, geotags, and optional spec tags (e.g., `is_qc_measurement: true`).
   * `exporter_internal_note` vs `buyer_facing_note` (separating factory floor notes from polished buyer communication).

## 5. Pan-India Go-To-Market (GTM) Rollout Sequencing

* **Phase 1 (Month 1): The Golden Triangle Test (Jaipur ➔ Jodhpur)**
  * Start in Jaipur (textiles) and Jodhpur (furniture). Both are in Rajasthan, share similar business cultural norms, and have high export densities.
* **Phase 2 (Month 2–3): The Uttar Pradesh Craft Belt (Moradabad ➔ Saharanpur ➔ Agra ➔ Bhadohi)**
  * Leverage EPCH regional chapters. One visit to the IHGF Delhi Fair lets you meet 300+ exporters from these exact 4 clusters in a single venue!
* **Phase 3 (Month 4+): South & West Apparel/Industrial Hubs (Tirupur, Surat, Morbi)**
  * Transition to higher-volume industrial suppliers using API integrations.
